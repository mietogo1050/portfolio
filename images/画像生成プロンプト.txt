A minimalist linocut and block print illustration in the style of Makoto Kagoshima, monochromatic simple one tone (black ink on white background). A female web engineer with black semi-long hair and center-parted bangs, sitting and working on a laptop at a calm beach. Nearby, there is a small fluffy poodle, a cute little crab on the sand, and a small stack of programming books. Folk-art aesthetic, rustic hand-made texture, simple and clean compositions, high contrast.
